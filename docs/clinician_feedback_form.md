# Clinician feedback form

**For:** physiotherapists, rehabilitation physicians or gait-lab staff reviewing the prototype.
**Time:** about 20 minutes.
**Data:** the prototype uses open research data, and no patient here is under your care.

Please open `prototype/gait_review.html` and look at patients **1079** (knee), **1750** (ankle), **613** (hip) and **212** (heel); each has four visits. Switch the change threshold between *Corrected* and *Original* at least once.

**Your role and years in practice:** ____________________

## A. Understanding (answer before reading the help text)

1. In your own words, what does the shaded band on the chart show?
2. What does the bar through each visit dot show?
3. For patient 1079, what changed between the first and last visit?
4. What does a review prompt ask you to do?

## B. Clinical meaning

5. The corrected threshold flags a change in loading asymmetry of about **0.085 body weights** or more between two visits. For a patient with your typical injury, is a smaller decline, say 0.05 body weights, one you would want to know about?
   ☐ Yes, always ☐ Sometimes ☐ No ☐ Cannot judge from this measure
6. The original threshold prompts about **1 patient in 5** for "lower than first visit". The corrected one prompts about **1 in 18**, but misses most smaller declines. Which would you rather have, and why?
7. What else would you need before acting on a prompt? (Examples: walking speed, pain, footwear, treatment changes.)
8. Could any wording on the page be read as a judgement about recovery, work capacity or compensation? Please quote it.

## C. Usefulness

Rate each statement: 1 = strongly disagree, 5 = strongly agree.

9. What one change would make this most useful to you?
10. What, if anything, worries you about a tool like this?

## Use of responses

Responses are anonymous and summarised in the project report. A response that disagrees with the design is as useful as one that agrees.
