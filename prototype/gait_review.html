GaitRec Rehab Review

Force-plate walking measurements during the first rehabilitation stay, for patients of the rehabilitation centre of the Austrian Workers' Compensation Board (AUVA), from the public GaitRec dataset (2007–2018). De-identified research data.

Research prototype. These are walking-force measurements, not a diagnosis or an assessment of recovery, work capacity, causation or impairment. Walking speed was not recorded and can change these values. Interpret only alongside the treating clinician's assessment.
