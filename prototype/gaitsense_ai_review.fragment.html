    Clinical recovery prediction (Capability 2)

    Status: built and tested on labelled synthetic data only. No clinical results.

    This panel will compare clinical-only, biomechanics-only and combined models for an independently measured recovery outcome (for example a validated function score or return to work) once a linked dataset has been verified. GaitRec has no such outcomes, so nothing here predicts recovery. Everything above describes force measurements only.

    Monitoring architectures: A raw asymmetry (limb symmetry index change ≥ 10 points), B healthy reference (crossing the healthy controls' 95% band), C statistical change limit (|z| > 1.96 including between-visit variation τ, which mixes measurement variation and genuine change), D predictive (observed value outside the GP's 95% predictive range).
