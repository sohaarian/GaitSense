01 / The platform

What is GaitSense?

GaitSense is an open-source research platform for examining repeated force-plate measurements and studying how walking biomechanics change between rehabilitation visits.

01 / MEASUREMENT

Observe gait

Explore force measurements, braking and propulsion, stance duration, and differences between limbs.

02 / PREDICTION

Model trajectories

Compare biomechanical predictions with measured values and inspect their uncertainty intervals.

03 / MONITORING

Review changes

Examine unusual visit-to-visit changes as prompts for research review, not clinical conclusions.

03 / Research status

Research software, not a clinical diagnosis.

GaitSense analyzes biomechanical measurements. Its clinical recovery prediction framework has been tested using synthetic data but has not been clinically validated. The platform must not be used for diagnosis, treatment, impairment ratings, maximum medical improvement, insurance eligibility, or return-to-work decisions.
